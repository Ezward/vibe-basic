================================================================================
PIRATE ISLAND - COMPLETE WALKTHROUGH
================================================================================

Final Score: 210/200 points — Rating: Sea Captain

OVERVIEW
--------
You wake aboard the pirate ship "The Eagle's Nest." Your goal is to find the
legendary Spanish gold coin hidden on Pirate Island and return it to the ship.

The game has three phases:
  1. Prepare on the ship (gather items, research coordinates, meet captain)
  2. Explore Pirate Island (collect key, pick, shovel; navigate caves)
  3. Retrieve the coin and return to the ship

STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
---------------------

=== PHASE 1: SHIP PREPARATION ===

Your Cabin (Room 26):
  OPEN DRAWER       — Reveals your pirate book and sword
  GET BOOK           — Pick up the book (has coordinate clues)
  GET SWORD          — Pick up the sword
  OPEN DOOR          — Opens passage east to hallway

Navigate to the crow's nest to get the shovel:
  E                  — Hallway (Room 27)
  S                  — Hallway (Room 31)
  U                  — Mid Deck (Room 33)
  CLIMB MAST         — Crow's Nest (Room 35) — shovel is here
  GET SHOVEL         — (+15 points)

Navigate to the captain's quarters:
  D                  — Back to Mid Deck (Room 33)
  N                  — Port Deck (Room 29) — captain's door is here
  KNOCK              — "What do you want to knock on?"
  DOOR               — Captain ushers you in (Room 25)

The captain asks for the coordinates of Pirate Island:
  29                 — Longitude (the book says 24, but Julius was off by 5°)
  45                 — Latitude (as written in the book)

  NOTE: The book says longitude 24, latitude 45. But research in the
  ship's library reveals Julius had a vision problem causing him to mark
  positions 5 degrees west of the true location. So: 24 + 5 = 29.

=== PHASE 2: WAIT FOR CAPTAIN (10 MOVES) ===

After giving correct coordinates, the captain needs 10 turns to sail to the
island. Navigate around the ship to kill time:

  S                  — Port Deck (Room 33)
  S                  — Starboard Deck (Room 39)
  D                  — Hallway (Room 37)
  W                  — Mess Hall (Room 36)
  S                  — Galley (Room 40) — note the cook and garlic here
  N                  — Mess Hall (Room 36)
  E                  — Hallway (Room 37)
  U                  — Starboard Deck (Room 39)
  N                  — Mid Deck (Room 33)

On the 10th move, the captain arrives and congratulates you for finding
Pirate Island. He gives you a bag of silver and opens the starboard deck
path to the island.

=== PHASE 3: BUY GARLIC ===

Navigate to the galley to buy garlic (needed to survive the bat cave):
  S                  — Starboard Deck (Room 39)
  D                  — Hallway (Room 37)
  W                  — Mess Hall (Room 36)
  S                  — Galley (Room 40)
  BUY GARLIC         — "With what?" prompt:
  BAG                — Trade the bag of silver for garlic

=== PHASE 4: GO ASHORE ===

  N                  — Mess Hall (Room 36)
  E                  — Hallway (Room 37)
  U                  — Starboard Deck (Room 39)
  S                  — Shallow Cove (Room 22) — (+20 points, now on the island)

=== PHASE 5: GET THE KEY ===

Navigate through the island to the tree where the key is hidden:
  N                  — Sandy Beach (Room 17)
  W                  — Beach Path (Room 16)
  N                  — Meadow (Room 12)
  W                  — Inland Cove Shore (Room 11)
  N                  — Base of Cliff (Room 6)
  D                  — Rain Forest (Room 3)
  E                  — Small Path (Room 4) — large tree here
  U                  — Up A Tree (Room 8) — key is here
  GET KEY            — Pick up the shiny key

=== PHASE 6: GET THE BOX AND PICK ===

Navigate to the inland cove and swim to the underwater area:
  D                  — Small Path (Room 4)
  W                  — Rain Forest (Room 3)
  U                  — Base of Cliff (Room 6)
  W                  — Inland Cove Shore (Room 11)
  SWIM               — Top of the Bay (Room 44)
  D                  — Bottom of the Bay (Room 45) — box is here
  GET BOX            — Pick up the small box
  OPEN BOX           — Reveals a thieve's pick inside
  DROP BOX           — Free up inventory space
  GET PICK           — Pick up the thieve's pick (+40 points)

=== PHASE 7: NAVIGATE TO THE BAT CAVE ===

Return to shore and take the long path through the swamps:
  U                  — Top of the Bay (Room 44)
  E                  — Inland Cove Shore (Room 11)
  E                  — Meadow (Room 12)
  S                  — Beach Path (Room 16)
  E                  — Sandy Beach (Room 17)
  E                  — Swamp (Room 18)
  N                  — Thick Forest (Room 13)
  N                  — Thick Forest (Room 9)
  N                  — Thick Forest (Room 5)
  E                  — Thick Forest (Room 10)
  S                  — Swamp (Room 19)
  N                  — Swamp (Room 14)
  W                  — Dark Cave (Room 20)
  E                  — Bat Cave (Room 21) — garlic keeps bats at bay!

=== PHASE 8: DIG UP THE GRATE AND FIND THE TREASURE ===

  DIG                — "With what?" prompt:
  SHOVEL             — Dig in damp soil, discover a metal grate!
  UNLOCK GRATE       — "With what?" prompt:
  PIC                — Use the thieve's pick — grate opens, passage down revealed
  D                  — Secret Cave (Room 23) — (+20 points) — pirate chest is here!
  UNLOCK CHEST       — "With what?" prompt:
  KEY                — Use the key — chest opens, revealing the Spanish gold coin!
  GET COIN           — Pick up the gold coin (+60 points)

=== PHASE 9: RETURN TO THE SHIP WITH THE COIN ===

Navigate back through the caves and swamps to the shore:
  U                  — Bat Cave (Room 21)
  W                  — Dark Cave (Room 20)
  W                  — Swamp (Room 14)
  S                  — Swamp (Room 19)
  S                  — Swamp (Room 18)
  W                  — Sandy Beach (Room 17)
  S                  — Shallow Cove (Room 22)
  S                  — Starboard Deck (Room 39) — VICTORY!

The captain greets you: "Welcome back, matey! Congradulations, you found
the coin. Now we will all be rich!" (+55 points)

WARNINGS / DEATH TRAPS
-----------------------
  - Room 15 (Quicksand): Reached from Room 16 W or Room 11 S — instant death
  - Room 43 (Underwater): Going E/W/U/D from Room 22 — instant death
  - Room 1 (High Cliff): Digging here triggers a booby-trapped coffin — death
  - Room 21 (Bat Cave): Entering without garlic — bats kill you
  - Room 40 (Galley): Trying to GET GARLIC without buying — cook kills you
  - Captain: Giving wrong coordinates — captain decapitates you after 10 moves
  - Room 39 (return): Coming back without the coin — captain kills you
